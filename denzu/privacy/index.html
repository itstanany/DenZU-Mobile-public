DenZU the clinic companion for dental students, interns and freelancers

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026 · applies to the DenZU Android and iOS apps

The short version. Your patients, cases, visits, media and notes stay on your device. We have no server that receives them. Two kinds of usage data are optional, ads are non-personalised, and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked before any ad is requested.

1. Who we are

DenZU is an independent app for dental students, interns and freelance dentists, published by an individual developer based in Egypt. In this policy "we" means that developer; "the app" means DenZU on Android and iOS.

Contact for anything in this policy, including data requests: ahmadalymohamad@gmail.com.

2. What never leaves your device

Patient names and identifiers, case records, diagnoses, procedures, treatment notes, photographs, audio recordings and any other clinical content stay in the app's local database on your device. There is no account system that syncs them and no mechanism in the app that transmits them.

If you export a case yourself (share sheet, file, screenshot), that copy leaves the app by your action and under your control, not ours.

3. What we collect, and only by your choice

Two switches, both in Settings → Privacy, both changeable at any time.

WhatDefaultWhat it contains
Product analytics Off Which screens are opened, where setup stops, which actions complete. No patient data, no case text, no names, no free text.
Crash and diagnostics On The stack trace and device model when the app crashes, so the crash can be fixed. No patient data.

Analytics events are keyed to a random identifier generated on your device. It is not your name, email, phone number or advertising identifier, and it is not linked to any account because the app has none.

If you turn analytics off, the identifier is kept for 30 days only, so that a deletion request made in that window can still name the data to delete. After 30 days it is erased.

4. Advertising

DenZU shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are what keep the app free.

  • Ads are requested as non-personalised ads. We do not ask AdMob to use your interests or a profile of you to choose them.
  • An ad request carries the technical data any request carries: IP address, device type, operating system and app version, and a device or advertising identifier. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's privacy policy.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks you before any ad request is made, and you can refuse. If you refuse, the app serves no ads at all, and you can change the choice later through the same message.
  • On iOS, the system asks whether the app may track you across other apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). Denying it does not remove ads; it removes access to the advertising identifier.
  • Ads never receive your clinical content. The ad code is separate from the case book and sees only which screen an ad slot sits on.

6. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete the case or uninstall the app. You control this entirely.
  • Analytics events: for as long as the analytics console retains them for reporting — currently configured retention, reviewed yearly.
  • The analytics identifier after you opt out: 30 days, for the deletion request described below.
  • Crash reports: 90 days (Crashlytics' default retention).

7. Your rights

You can ask us to confirm what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict how it is used, or send it to you in a portable form. Write to ahmadalymohamad@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

To delete analytics data: open Settings → Privacy → Delete my analytics data, copy the identifier shown, and send it to the address above. What was collected under that identifier is removed from the analytics console by hand, within 30 days. Ad data is held by Google and is not part of that request — Google's own controls apply to it. Nothing on the device can revoke a copy a provider already holds, which is why the request names the identifier.

If you are in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

8. Children

DenZU is intended for dental professionals and students aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete what we hold.

9. Security

The case book is stored in the app's private storage, protected by the operating system's sandbox, and the app can be locked with the device's biometrics or passcode. Exported files are protected by whatever you share them into.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the app will say so before the change takes effect and the date at the top will move. The current version is always at this address.
